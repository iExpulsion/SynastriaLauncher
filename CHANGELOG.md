# Changelog

## 1.0.13

### Patches

- **Nameplate Unit Tokens:** A new Experimental patch in the Nameplates group that lets nameplate addons read every
  nameplate, not just your target's. Addons that look for it, such as DragonUI, then show health, auras, cast bars
  and quest icons on every nameplate instead of only your target, mouseover and focus. Don't use it together with
  AwesomeWotLK.
